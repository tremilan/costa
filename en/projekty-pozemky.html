Testimonials

“For me, Costa Rica is rightly called the Switzerland of Central America: a peaceful country with no army, which invests in people’s education and in protecting nature instead of weapons. A relatively small state between two oceans offers rainforests, volcanoes and clean beaches that are easy to fall in love with. Local people live by the philosophy of pura vida, and they are among the happiest in the world. It is no surprise that many people come back to make a new home here, and a quiet place to retire.”

Marek Vašut Project ambassador · actor and entrepreneur

“On our own land we quite literally have a rainforest: monkeys, parrots, toucans and tropical butterflies. We wanted to live in step with nature, and Costa Rica, as a unique ecosystem, was the obvious choice. With a spring on the land itself, the sun and fertile soil, we are building a self-sufficient home only a few minutes from the sea. Simply pura vida.”

Marek Popálený Investor · entrepreneur

“My house, with a large terrace, is being built now, and I like both the project as a whole and the honest building materials. With friends, we have a place to retire to here, should we need it.”
