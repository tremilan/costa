The location

What keeps the Osa Peninsula exceptional

Two reasons the supply of land on Osa is limited.

A limited opportunity

The main driver of prices on Osa is that the state now allows almost nobody to build anything new. If you own a legally clean property on a peninsula that holds 2.5% of the world’s biodiversity, you hold an exceptionally rare and highly valued asset.

Growth in tourism

In the first quarter of 2026, for the first time, more than 1 million visitors came to the country in a single quarter (1,033,777). That is 11.3% more than in the same period of 2025.
