Our story

Reality Kostarika grew out of a personal love of the Osa Peninsula, and from the belief that Czech investors deserve the same standard of service as when they buy a premium property at home. We build gated estates in and around Puerto Jiménez. We have sold more than 100 villas here, and we now look after their management and letting in full.

Martin Barták

Partner, CEO, Development

Building the estates, strategic leadership and development on the Osa Peninsula.

Martin spent years looking after investors’ money, and he reads world markets through hard data. Costa Rica caught him with both his head and his heart. He saw an unusually stable investment, and an ideal retreat outside Europe. Today he helps other people build a Plan B in a place he thinks of as his second home.

Timea Trajtelová

Partner, CMO, Development

Marketing, client communication and the public face of Reality Kostarika.

Timea has business and an international setting in her blood, and development has always been a passion. After building her own fashion brand, she found a second home abroad. Today she lives in Costa Rica for about half the year. She does not pass clients dry information. She shares a real, everyday experience of life in the tropics.

Pavel Friedrich

Partner, CSO, CTO

Technology, processes and the long-term vision for the estate.

Pavel has spent his working life in technology, IT and the strategic management of large companies. His first trip to Costa Rica showed him a quite different kind of calm and freedom. The nature, and the business potential of the Osa Peninsula, spoke to him so strongly that he joined our closest team. He found a second home in our estate, where he now enjoys his own base with his family.

The team in the Czech Republic

A full client and advisory service, entirely in Czech. Our job is to make an investment in an exotic place as comfortable and as clear as possible. We stay with you from the first plans through to handover of the property and its place in the rental system.

The Costa Rican team

Our local architects, civil engineers, managers and the wider support team, on the Osa Peninsula itself. Years of being based in the region give us full control of the property cycle, from the first architectural design through a precise build to the professional day-to-day management of the estate.

The company’s partners

Our long-term partners include significant firms working in Prague and in Costa Rica. They provide a professional service with a personal approach. Thanks to them, we are the largest Czech property company operating in Costa Rica.

Prague Property Management

For more than 30 years, PSN has been among the largest Prague businesses in property. The portfolio includes houses, flats, residences and commercial space. We have a long relationship with the company, and we have always been satisfied with its leadership and its services.

Monika Jirásková, law firm

For more than 10 years, Monika Jirásková’s firm has represented large developers and estate agencies on the Czech market. A specialist team of lawyers and tax advisers guides clients through Czech and Costa Rican law, solicitor’s escrow and contracts drawn up for the individual case.

KLB Legal

KLB Legal advises on contracts, from template documents and managing signatures through to legal communication with clients. We also have an excellent experience of the firm in capital markets.

Century 21 Czech Republic

CENTURY 21 is the world’s leading property network, with nearly 14,250 offices in 86 countries. We have an exclusive agreement with the leadership of C21 in the Czech Republic to sell our developments on the Czech market.

Sotheby's International Costa Rica

Sotheby's International Realty® leads the sale and letting of exceptional property. We have a close personal relationship with the Costa Rican office, and we share a standard of premium service.

ČSOB Bank

Through 4fin Better together and ČSOB, we arrange exclusive lending terms for clients who can offer a property in the Czech Republic as security. Clients can then repay the loan from the villa’s rental income.

UniCredit Bank

UniCredit Bank Czech Republic and Slovakia is part of the Europe-wide UniCredit group. It serves retail clients, business owners and larger investors, including private banking. We have worked together since the days of Živnostenská banka.

Navarro & Vargas, building firm

The Costa Rican building company Navarro & Vargas has long experience of residential construction. With a team of experienced engineers, each project is delivered to the agreed timetable and to the satisfaction of everyone involved.

Banco de Costa Rica

Banco de Costa Rica is the largest state-owned commercial bank in Costa Rica, and one of the strongest banks in Central America. We have worked together from the start, and personal links with the management mean that approving investments is not held up.

Global ESG & Sustainability Summit
